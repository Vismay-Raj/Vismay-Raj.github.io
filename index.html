Project insights

What the listing data suggests

Takeaways to guide careful analysis

Sales field82.9% numeric

35,381 rows have numeric sales values. The remaining entries include text labels or scraped fragments, so they should not be treated as sales quantities.

CoveragePrices need a subset view

Current price is present for 72.5% of rows, while ratings are present for 97.6%. Price comparisons should use the available-price subset and disclose the missing coverage.

Titles and repeatsDo not assume product duplicates

There are 8,034 distinct cleaned titles. Repeated full rows can be counted, but without stable product IDs or capture dates they do not establish unique products or time trends.
